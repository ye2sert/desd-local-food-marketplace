# T3 – Stakeholder Analysis

**Project:** Bristol Regional Food Network – Digital Marketplace

**Owner:** Tahmid  
**Sprint:** 1  
**Points:** 3

## Stakeholders and Their Needs

The Bristol Regional Food Network Digital Marketplace connects local food producers with customers through an online platform.

The main stakeholders are customers, producers, the marketplace platform and marketplace administrators. External services include payment and delivery services.

### 1. Customers

Customers use the marketplace to browse and purchase products from different local producers.

**Needs:**
- Browse and search available food products.
- Add products from multiple producers to one basket.
- Check product availability before checkout.
- Make secure payments.
- Receive order confirmations.
- Access delivery or collection information.

### 2. Local Food Producers

Producers supply food products through the marketplace.

**Needs:**
- Manage product listings and stock quantities.
- Receive orders from customers.
- Receive notifications when orders are confirmed.
- Access information about their orders.
- Receive weekly payment settlements.
- View payment and commission records.

### 3. Marketplace Platform

The marketplace platform coordinates interactions between customers, producers and external services.

**Responsibilities:**
- Manage product and stock information.
- Validate customer baskets.
- Create and split multi-vendor orders.
- Coordinate payment processing.
- Update stock after successful payments.
- Calculate the marketplace's 5% commission.
- Notify producers about confirmed orders.

### 4. Marketplace Administrators

Marketplace administrators oversee marketplace operations.

**Needs:**
- Monitor orders and transactions.
- Review payment failures.
- Access commission and settlement information.
- Maintain accurate marketplace records.

## External Services

### 5. Payment Service

The marketplace depends on an external or simulated payment service to process customer payments.

**Responsibilities:**
- Process payment requests.
- Return successful or declined payment responses.
- Provide payment confirmation.
- Support payment retry or exception handling.

A sandbox or simulated payment service will be used for assessment purposes.

### 6. Delivery Service

Delivery services may support the transportation of products from producers to customers.

**Responsibilities:**
- Support delivery arrangements.
- Provide delivery information.
- Help coordinate the fulfilment of customer orders.

## Conclusion

Identifying stakeholders and external services helps the team understand how different participants interact with the marketplace.

This analysis will support the strategic BPMN model and later stakeholder dependency modelling.
